# SP2 design review 06: the interaction design lens (first draft of the plan, 2026-10-05, at b69ff0c)

VERDICT: The plan is careful about data fidelity and labels, but through the product lens it does not yet deliver the user's main ask, to SEE the launch: with the shared true-scale camera and the replay's pinned Auto rate the rocket is under 2 px wide after 28% of the playback and an unspecified icon for the last half, the pad's kick moves the nose 1.2 px, and staging, stage-2 ignition and the fairing drop pass in 1.5 wall seconds at 290 to 660 m per pixel, while no exit criterion measures visibility. The most important change is a fixed-scale vehicle close-up in each panel, an event-aware playback rate (which needs D-SP2-21 amended) and a "watchable" exit criterion; after that, specify what the page shows when a launch completes without a trajectory, the one-run and default-pair layouts, and per-run caveats in the results panel. The two mock-ups named in step A0's gate were not on disk for this review (not in C:/Users/rahul/.claude/plans or the session scratchpad), so the layout points below should be checked against them.

## 1. [blocker] The vehicle is not legible for most of the playback, and no gate measures it

CLAIM: As specified, the user sees the rocket's attitude and plume for about the first quarter of the playback only, cannot see the pad's kick, staging or the fairing halves at all on a true-scale view, and every exit criterion would still pass because criterion 5 reads state values, not visibility.

EVIDENCE: Computed from results/silo_offload_2d/20261003T112934Z (pad beside silo_cold_s1, the D-SP2-28 default) with the camera law of plan 4.8 on a 520 px panel and the Auto rate of src/launchsim/templates/replay.html 509-515 (script: C:/Users/rahul/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/D--DEV-ClaudeProjects-SpaceRocketOptimization/6999a833-8705-4f59-babb-7d35feacc46d/scratchpad/a0/review/ux_timeline.py). Start: view 260 m, 2.0 px/m, rocket 140 x 7.3 px. Body width under 3 px from T+4.7 s (wall 7.3 s, 19% of the 37.6 s playback), under 2 px from T+7.8 s (wall 10.4 s, 28%), under 1 px from T+14.8 s; the plan's icon switch (length under 6 px, plan 4.4 and 4.8) comes only at T+32.7 s (wall 18.7 s, 50%). Pad kick at T+12.49 s: -2.83 deg on a rocket 23 px long and 1.2 px wide, so the nose moves 1.2 px (silo run: -4.13 deg on 122 px, 8.8 px, in one frame at the ignition instant). MECO and staging fall at wall 24.3 and 24.75 s at 291 to 370 m/px; the 11 s staging coast lasts 0.37 wall s; staging to fairing 1.5 wall s; fairing at 577 to 659 m/px. Survey 09 section 2.4: the spent stage is within 0.2 m for 11 s and 284 m away at +20 s (under 1 px); section 7 item 4: both fairing halves follow one path. Plan 4.5 lists no trace of the flown path; 4.8 does not say whether the icon keeps attitude, plume or stage count, or how the spent stage and fairing (0.1 px at that scale) are drawn.

CHANGE: (a) Add to plan 4.5 and 4.8 a vehicle close-up in each panel: a fixed-scale inset (the 70 m stack about 160 px long, local horizontal level, a vertical reference line, a pitch readout) drawn by the same vehicle routine from the same state, showing attitude, plume, tank fills, the stage-1 drop with a drawn gap over the staging coast and the fairing halves opening, each marked "drawn, not computed" as 4.10 requires. (b) Main view: true scale while the body is at least 3 px wide (not "length under 6 px"), then a position marker with a heading tick instead of an enlarged rocket; the flown path as a solid trace with event markers; spent stage and fairing as hollow markers on dashed display-only paths (they clear the vehicle marker by about 11 px two wall seconds after staging at 30x, 38 px after four). (c) New exit criterion "Watchable", read through the state hook for pad beside silo_cold_s1 and pad beside silo_cold: close-up vehicle length at least 120 px at every time; the nose moves at least 4 px at each run's kick; staging, stage-2 ignition and the fairing drop each stay on screen at least 1.5 wall s under Auto; separated bodies are drawn at least 4 px across. Cost: (b) is cheap inside A3 and A6; (a) is one more call of the vehicle routine with a fixed transform plus QA rows in A7 (moderate). Cheaper fallback if (a) is declined: draw the vehicle at max(true scale, 56 px) with a magnification badge; it keeps attitude and plume but overstates the rocket beside the to-scale silo.

## 2. [major] The pinned Auto rate makes the events unwatchable; D-SP2-21 forbids fixing it

CLAIM: D-SP2-21 copies currentRate verbatim, so the scene inherits thresholds tuned for graphs: the part specific to launch assist is over in 5 wall seconds, the view lurches at T+45 s, and staging and the fairing drop run at 30x.

EVIDENCE: src/launchsim/templates/replay.html 509-515 (1x below 12 s, 5x below 45 s, 30x after), 516-525 (tick has no cap on the frame interval), 547-550 (Space is the only key); D-SP2-21 pins currentRate, tick and setPlaying by a source-comparison test. Computed (ux_timeline.py): push, release, ignition and ramp (T-2.6 to T+2.5 s) take 5.1 of 37.6 wall s, the 2.6 s push itself 7%; the pad's kick plays at 5x (1.06 wall s); the view scale changes x3.3 to x4.5 in one wall second where the rate steps to 30x (survey 09 section 4.3 gives 3.2 to 4.5), with max-Q 0.3 to 0.7 wall s later; MECO to fairing is 1.5 wall s. The scrubber steps 0.1 s per arrow key over about 5,400 steps. Survey 04 section 1.5: after a hidden tab the next frame advances by the whole absence times the rate, and in the app the user tabs away during a 2 to 5 minute solve.

CHANGE: Amend D-SP2-21: pin the seven other helpers and give the scene its own currentRate, a pure function of scene time and the shown runs' events: 0.5x from the first row to the last stage-1 ramp end; 1x to the last kick end; a smooth geometric ramp to 30x with no step; held at 3x from 2 s before the first MECO to 3 s after the last stage-2 ignition, and within 3 s of each fairing drop and of cutoff or impact (roughly 55 to 60 s for the default pair against 38 s; 20x and 60x stay for a quick pass). Add 0.25x and 0.5x to the rate select (these work even with the verbatim function, which returns Number(sel)). Add previous-event and next-event buttons and keys ([ and ]), Left and Right for 1 s, Shift for 10 s, and clickable ticker entries. Pause on visibilitychange and auto-start only when the document is visible (a listener outside tick, compatible with the pin). Expose rateAt(t) in the state hook. All cheap, in A3 and A6.

## 3. [major] A launch that completes without a trajectory has no defined screen

CLAIM: A failed search writes a normal directory with summary.md and an empty time series, which the plan's states, refusal list and results panel do not cover, so after a wait of one to several minutes the user gets unspecified behaviour.

EVIDENCE: src/launchsim/sim.py 1346-1385: search_failed_result returns "empty planar time series and events" with status search_failed and raises nothing, so no FAILED.txt is written; src/launchsim/replay.py 374-375 raises "is empty (a failed search writes no trajectory)" for such a run. Reachable from the form: a height by event within about 0.41 m below the drag-free apex (phase file section 5.7, lines 649-652: "The app shows it as a failed run with its reason"; survey 03 section 3.3: at_height_m 300.9 passes the preflight) and a hot start on a gentle push (survey 03 section 3.3: drive_limit, a hand estimate of below about 0.36 g0 net). Plan 4.4 refuses only 1-D, sweep, FAILED.txt and missing summary; 4.6 keys "failed" on FAILED.txt; 4.7 covers only the flagged stage-2 case; exit criterion 2 says "its scene plays"; the phase file's failed-run sentence was not carried into the plan.

CHANGE: Add to plan 4.6 and 4.7 an outcome table with what the page shows for each: refused (422: message under the field, nothing written); crashed (FAILED.txt: its last line, no scene); complete but the variant did not fly (search_failed or guidance_failed: panel headed "The run did not fly" with the failure kind and message from the run's flags, the pad shown alone in one panel, browser row "complete, variant failed: <kind>"); complete but not in orbit (impact, short_of_orbit: the scene plays under a status banner); complete and flagged (flags listed above the numbers); offload ended no_offload or its solve failed. The payload builder marks a run with zero rows no_trajectory instead of raising, and the job state gains done_with_failures. Add a fast test on the fixed-guidance basis and one with at_height_m in the band; reword exit criterion 2 to "its scene plays, or the page says why there is none". Cheap, in A4 and A5.

## 4. [major] One-run scenes, the default pair and run selection in a 19-run directory are unspecified

CLAIM: The plan defines the scene only as two panels and the default only for offload directories, so a pad-only launch, the calibration directories and the screening directory have no defined view, and the inherited default would put a yardstick pad beside the pad.

EVIDENCE: Results listing (script .../scratchpad/a0/review/ux_dirs.py): results/calibration_f9_2d/20260930T100100Z and 20260930T173928Z have runs [pad] plus 7 case folders; results/silo_screening_2d/20260930T175743Z and results/silo_bridge_2d_readme/20260930T185034Z list runs as pad, pad_instant, silo_instant, silo_cold, so the inherited default (src/launchsim/plots.py 645-658: the baseline plus the first three variants; survey 05 section 8) has pad_instant, an M5 yardstick, second; results/silo_offload_2d/20261003T112934Z has 4 runs and 15 offload runs (3 pad controls, 1 paired pad, 11 cases). Plan: D-SP2-28 covers only a directory with an offload block; 4.8 says "Up to four runs per directory, two panels shown, chips to switch" without saying which two, which panel a chip replaces or how four are picked from 19; 4.7 "pad only sends no variant" produces a one-run directory.

CHANGE: Add to plan 4.8: (1) default pair for every directory: left is the baseline; right is the first solved stage-1 case when an offload block exists, else the first variant with an assist that is not a yardstick (replay.is_yardstick, src/launchsim/replay.py 563), else none. (2) One-run layout: a single panel at full width with its own camera and no empty second panel; the results panel says "pad baseline: every launch is compared with this run". (3) Replace "chips to switch" by one run selector per panel, options grouped by role (experiment runs, offload cases, paired pads, pad controls, cases, bounds) with the run's note (offload_note) as the option text; the app reloads the scene for the chosen pair, so the four-run limit stops being the user's concern. Cheap, in A5 and A6.

## 5. [major] The results panel has no specified content, and two of plan 4.10's fixed caveats are false for recorded presets

CLAIM: The plan does not say which numbers the panel shows or in what order, and 4.10 fixes as text "beside every number" two statements that are wrong for some runs, and puts SP1's 41.26 t beside numbers from other configurations with nothing saying they differ.

EVIDENCE: Plan 4.6 says only that the panel is built from the directory; 4.10: "no structural mass for the 4 g push (an assumed +8.1 t leaves 1.98 t of SP1's 41.26 t), the offloaded run's max-Q above the pad's". Recorded data (script .../scratchpad/a0/review/ux_caveats.py on results/silo_offload_2d/20261003T112934Z/metrics.json): vs_pad.max_q_above_pad is false on 4 of the 11 cases (silo_cold_s1_dry+2t, +4t, +8.1t and silo_cold_fix5pct, each a preset of plan 4.7); felt_g_track_peak is 3.996 on silo_cold but 2.496 on silo_cold_200m (also a preset); the directory's own caveat 4 is neutral ("reported beside the pad's"). A custom depth or acceleration returns a number next to SP1's headline, which reads as a new finding.

CHANGE: Specify the panel in plan 4.7. Line 1: the tag "Exploratory app run: not a finding" (or "Recorded: <experiment>/<timestamp>, git <hash>"). Line 2: one headline per launch kind (payload capacity and its difference from the pad; or propellant removed at the pad's payload in t and % of stage 1; or P* - P_ref for a fixed case; stage 2 and both net of the pad control). Line 3: "Same configuration as SP1's <preset>: recorded <value>, this run <value>" when the form equals a preset, else "SP1's recorded case is 100 m at 3 g net: 41.26 t; this launch differs in: <fields>". Then the push (exit speed, felt g, interface force, energy, peak power, facility length), the flight (liftoff mass, MECO, max-Q with "above" or "below the pad's" taken from vs_pad.max_q_above_pad), losses, flags, verification. Generate the caveats per run: the structure caveat quotes this run's felt_g_track_peak, the max-Q sentence states this run's own comparison, and the +8.1 t sentence belongs to SP1's line only. Displayed precision: tonnes to 0.01, kg to 1. A5's honesty review then has a specification to check against.

## 6. [major] The form is specified in config keys, with no units, derived values or disabled states, and the penalty field can do nothing on a full load

CLAIM: Plan 4.7 is a key mapping, not a form design: a user who does not know the keys gets no units, limits or derived numbers, refusals arrive in key vocabulary with no field attached, and the structural penalty is offered as an independent group although the config has it only on an offload case.

EVIDENCE: Plan 4.7's field column is the keys (stroke_m, net_accel_g, stage1_dry_mass_added_t, both_fraction) with two disabled states in total; POST /api/validate (plan 4.6) has no stated response. src/launchsim/config.py 1637: stage1_dry_mass_added_t is a field of the offload case only, so with "full load" selected the penalty has nowhere to go and the plan does not say what happens. Real refusal texts (survey 03 section 3.1): "ignition stage1: at_depth_m 150 m is deeper than the track (stroke_m 100 m)"; "run 'silo': at_height_m 301.5 m is at or above the drag-free apex v_e^2 / (2 g_eff) = 301.060928 m"; pydantic's "Input should be greater than 0" names its field only in loc. Survey 03 section 6: a text field showing 76.70717 and re-read no longer equals the 200 m preset's 76.70717046013364. Plan 4.6 measures resolve plus preflight at 9 ms; survey 03 section 5: "a validate-on-change endpoint is affordable".

CHANGE: Add to plan 4.7 a field table: a plain label with its unit and the config key in small type (so messages can be matched), default, allowed range, and a disabled-state matrix (pad only: push, silo details, push-relative ramp starts, offload and penalty disabled with the reason shown; full load: pre-offload, paired pad and penalty disabled with "applies to an offload case"; failed ignition: offload disabled; a penalty clears the paired pad; stage 2 or both: pad control locked on). Define the response of /api/validate as the derived block computed by the server from the resolved config and shown live: exit speed or net acceleration (whichever was not typed), push time, felt g, braking distance and facility length, the drag-free apex limit for a height, the ramp start expressed as time, depth, speed and height, the run names to be written and the expected duration; or the refusal with a field id (from loc or the rule) so the page marks the field (aria-invalid) and puts the one-line message under it. Untouched fields of a preset are sent as the server's stored values, never re-read from the text shown. First-open default: the silo_cold preset (a plain launch of about 50 s), not a solve. Preset buttons carry a plain name, the committed name beneath and the expected duration. Cheap, in A5.

## 9. [major] Video and export: the default name collides in normal use, and capture has no progress, stop or measured cost

CLAIM: Two videos from one directory want the same file under a new "never overwriting" rule, the user is not told where the file is or what happens during up to 1,800 frame uploads, and nothing in the survey measured how long capture takes or how large the GIF is.

EVIDENCE: Plan 4.9: `<experiment>_<timestamp>_scene.mp4` in the server's working directory, "never overwriting", plus GET /api/videos/<id>/file. The existing rule has neither run names nor an existence check: src/launchsim/replay.py 1209-1219 and 1237-1244 (write_text overwrites), src/launchsim/plots.py 980-993. No toBlob or per-frame timing appears in surveys 02 to 09; the animate GIFs on the site are 0.7 to 2.7 MB at far fewer frames. D-SP2-30's in-canvas HUD and caveat footer exist only on the composite canvas, so "no second renderer" does not cover them. The HTML export has no control in the app.

CHANGE: Plan 4.9: name the file `<experiment>_<timestamp>_<left>-vs-<right>_scene.<ext>` (run names are already valid file names) with -2, -3 on a clash, as make_run_dir does; write one copy to the working directory and show its absolute path with a copy button (keep the GET link as a convenience); show "frame n of N" with Cancel (the page stops posting, the server discards the partial file); the clip follows the player's Auto law, `seconds` defaults to its natural length and other values scale it uniformly; list the session's clips under their directory in the run browser. In A6v, before fixing the limits, measure ms per frame and file size for MP4 and GIF at 1280 px over the full clip; if the GIF passes about 20 MB, cap GIF at 12 fps and 960 px. Add "Download scene (HTML)" in the app (the frame's URL with a download attribute; the server writes nothing) beside the equivalent CLI line. A7's visual QA takes one video frame per theme for the in-canvas HUD.
