What each run carries to orbit

Payload capacity is the largest payload that still reaches the 200 km circular orbit with zero propellant left, found by the simulator's search. The ideal rocket-equation estimate for a 76.7 m/s head start, holding all losses constant, is about 765 kg.

Read before quoting these numbers

  • Planar 2-D model over a rotating spherical Earth, with drag and back-pressure. Guidance is sweep-optimized, not optimal control, and the engines never throttle.
  • The vehicle calibrates 14.3% high: it carries 26,054 kg from the pad against the published 22,800 kg. It was accepted as a documented miss, so read these numbers as differences between runs.
  • No structural mass is charged for the silo's load case: the fully fuelled stack feels about 4 g during the push. About 8.1 t of extra stage-1 structure would cancel the cold-start gain.
  • The silo drive is a prescribed 3 g push with no force or power limit, the carriage is massless, the shaft has no air drag, and the pitch kick has no aerodynamic penalty. Each of these favours the silo.
  • The instant-ignition runs are yardsticks, not designs: no real engine reaches full thrust at the instant of release.
