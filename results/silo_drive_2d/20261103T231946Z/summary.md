## Propellant saved at fixed payload

Offload basis: propellant saved at fixed payload. Different vehicles (each run's own propellant load, tanks partly filled, dry masses unchanged unless a row says so: a penalty row's assumed stage-1 dry mass, or a structural case's modelled increment), the same payload and the same orbit: every solved offload is measured at the reference payload P_ref, the full-load pad baseline's payload capacity P* on the same vehicle and orbit (sweep-optimized guidance, the shared search budget); an imposed (fixed) case flies its own payload capacity P*, read as P* - P_ref in its own sub-section, never as propellant saved. Reference payload P_ref = 26054.4 kg (pad's payload capacity P*).

Caveats (they travel with every number below):

- calibration: the vehicle generic_f9_class_2d carries 26,054 kg in its calibration run against the published 22,800 kg, +14.3% high, a documented calibration result (docs/findings/CAL-f9-leo-2d); read every offload as a difference between runs of the vehicle model, not as a Falcon 9 figure
- guidance is sweep-optimized (a shared gamma* grid refined per run, not optimal control) and the engines never throttle
- the structural cases charge the modelled structural mass of the push load on their assisted runs, a first-order station sizing of the push load (a first-order estimate with its stated ranges, not a detailed structural design;
