## 1. Context

SP1's headline: at the pad's payload P_ref = 26,054.396 kg and the 200 km orbit, the 3 g0,
100 m cold-start silo lets the gate vehicle leave out 41,262.908 kg of stage-1 propellant
(10.04% of stage 1), before any structural mass; assumed +2, +4, +8.1 t of stage-1 dry mass
leave 32.285, 22.876, 1.980 t (RQ1). SP7 charges a modelled structural mass for the push with
its uncertainty shown in three layers (a physics band, design-assumption axes, an outer
envelope), beside those rows, and builds the linear motor (force- and acceleration-controlled,
with carriage mass, a rise ramp, a stop taper, release at a target speed, braking functions,
a sealed-shaft air column).

What the surveys and reviews established that the design rests on:

- **Construction is public** (S06; SpaceX Falcon 9 User's Guide Rev 2 2015, Table 2-1 and
  section 2.2; Falcon User's Guide 2025): Al-Li skin, friction-stir welded; **LOX tank on top,
  monocoque; RP-1 tank below, skin and stringer; an insulated common dome between them**; a LOX
  transfer tube through the RP-1 tank; a composite interstage; the same construction wording
  for stage 2; 3.66 m; structural factor of safety 1.4. Tank pressures, dome shapes, tank
  lengths and a dry-mass breakdown are not public.
- **Where a quasi-static push exceeds the pad's own envelope** (S05): the LOX barrel in
  compression is inside it (push n U = 0.77 of MECO's 7.095 MN); the RP-1 barrel carries
  n (U + LOX) at 2.20 (offloaded headline) to 2.37 (full load) times its envelope (MECO); the
  load through the stage base is 3.7-4.0 times the hold-down support (5.593 MN); the tank-bottom
  heads are 2.6-3.0 times liftoff's. Below MECO's 5.195 g0 the push does not size stage 2; a
  dynamic overshoot above a factor 1.40, a 50 m stroke (7.0 g0) or a force-limited hot start
  does.
- **Magnitude (probe, S08; placeholders, no non-optimum factor):** a quasi-static tank
  increment of 1.4-3.5 t, plus 0.4-2.1 t of load-entry hardware at 20-100 kg/MN; on SP1's
  penalty curve 12-33 t of offload (30-80% of the headline) quasi-statically, 1.6-29.5 t at a
  dynamic factor of 1.3, 0-20.2 t at 2.0. S08 recommended reporting constant_accel at its
  as-flown factor 2. The ranges adopted here are less favourable than the probe's: a
  non-optimum factor of 1.54-1.9 on every increment (Wu 2024, coarse-model values), a combined
  hoop-and-compression mode (+0.4 t before that factor, review), and stage 2 sized wherever the
  push exceeds MECO. A planning reading at the central values is therefore an offload below
  half of SP1's 41.26 t, and the adverse ends may reach no offload at all. This is said now so
  that the result is not a surprise; it is not a target.
- **A power limit cannot lower the load** (S01, S07): at a fixed stroke and exit speed,
  constant force is the minimum-peak profile; the linear motor's relief comes only from a
  longer stroke or a lower exit speed. A rise ramp costs a little peak g at 100 m and more at
  short strokes (+1.1% at 100 m and +5.3% at 50 m for a 1.06 s ramp, review); a stop taper
  costs much more (+21% to +52% of the plateau force at 100 m, review).
- **The release is a load step for every drive.** The felt load drops from about 4 g0 to 0 in
  one instant; on the stage-1 tanks, which are full at release and nearly empty at MECO, the
  first-order check says the LOX column would separate from its dome (probe: -0.53 MPa
  absolute at the bottom).
- **Code seams** (S01-S04): a new drive plugs into `AssistModel.state_rate`; a vehicle transform
  inside `OffloadProblem.vehicle_at` keeps SP1's path unchanged when off; resolved dicts are raw
  merged YAML; the 1-D golden tier can never be recaptured and dumps every field of every
  dataclass it reaches, so no field may be added to an existing dataclass on that path (review
  N1); no test pins SP1's x* today (S03).

### 4.2 The structural model (structure.py)

**Stations and frame.** `station_height_m` runs up the stack from the load ring at the stage
base (its own frame, documented in physics.md; not the track frame's z). Elements, bottom to
top: aft skirt (length L_s), RP-1 aft dome, RP-1 barrel, common dome, LOX barrel, forward dome,
interstage, then stage 2's tanks in the same pattern; the transfer tube through the RP-1 tank.
Barrel lengths from the full-load propellant volumes at the stated densities plus an ullage
fraction, over A = pi r^2 (cylinder-equivalent; dome volumes neglected, stated), built once at
transform-build time and closed over (the push at any x is evaluated on the same stations as
the envelope). Each barrel has N stations (a test: doubling N moves dm by < 0.1 kg).

**Load case** (`LoadCase`, frozen): felt axial load factor n (g0), the stage-1 and stage-2
propellant on board split into LOX and RP-1 at each stage's mixture fraction (structure file),
the upper mass above stage 1 U (stage 2 wet + fairing if attached + payload flown), the interface
force F_int (push only), the thrust T, the phase. The liquid surface of a tank sits m/(rho A)
above its bottom; the head at a station is h = max(0, surface - station height). sim.py adapts
a Result (or, inside the transform, a flown push or the analytic constant_accel case) into load
cases; structure.py imports no I/O module.

**Station loads** (free body above a cut; the liquid of the tank whose wall is cut is carried by
its bottom dome, not by the wall above it; the other tank's ullage pressure is internal to the
free body; S05, SP-9):
- LOX barrel: N(z) = (U + m_s,above(z)) n g0; relief p_min,LOX pi r^2;
- RP-1 barrel: N(z) = (U + m_s,above(z) + m_LOX) n g0; relief p_min,RP1 pi r^2;
- aft skirt (unpressurized, no relief): N = the load through the base (push: F_int; pad: the
  hold-down support, or the flight thrust path as a discrete alternative in the physics band);
- stage 2 the same, with its own U (fairing and payload above it);
- pressure at a barrel station: p(z) = p_u + rho n g0 h(z), p_u the maximum expected operating
  pressure (MEOP) of that tank.
m_s,above from the structure file's breakdown (not from the sized thicknesses); the increment's
own inertia is not added (about 0.3% of dm, stated). Drag is left out of the envelope (it would
raise the envelope by about 0.01%, S05; leaving it out charges slightly more).

**Modes** (FS_u the ultimate factor, F_tu the ultimate strength, eta_w the weld efficiency on the
hoop direction; relief at the tank's minimum expected pressure, unfactored; NASA-STD-5001B
FSR 19 and 53-54):
- hoop: t_h = FS_u p(z) r / (F_tu eta_w);
- net design compression N_d = FS_u N(z) - p_min pi r^2 (none if N_d <= 0):
  - monocoque (LOX barrels): the smallest t with 2 pi E t^2 (gamma(t)/sqrt(3(1 - nu^2)) +
    s_dg Delta_gamma(p_min, t)) >= N_d; gamma = 1 - 0.901 (1 - exp(-sqrt(r/t)/16)) (SP-8007 1968
    Eq. 5; 2020 Eq. 9-10); Delta_gamma from the digitized SP-8007 Fig. 6 / Fig. 4-5 curve of
    (p/E)(r/t)^2 (2020 Eq. 48-49), evaluated at p_min only, linearly interpolated (monotone);
    solved by brentq to 1e-12 m;
  - stiffened (RP-1 barrels, aft skirt): smeared t_bar = (d/4) C (N_x/(k_s E d))^n,
    N_x = N_d/(2 pi r), k_s the stiffened knockdown, (C, n) one row of Gerard & Lakshmikantham
    1966 Table 2 chosen as a pair; a "plate-limited" alternative (thickness proportional to the
    load, anchored at the envelope load; Lovejoy 2010's 2195 trend) as the adverse row;
- combined (barrels): the smallest t with sqrt(s_h^2 - s_h s_x + s_x^2) <= F_tu eta_w, where
  s_h = FS_u p(z) r / t is the hoop stress and s_x = -N_d / (2 pi r t) the net axial membrane
  stress at ultimate (negative in net compression), so a compressive axial stress raises the
  combined stress;
- minimum gauge t_min.
t(z, c) = the largest of these. Domes are membranes sized at the crown, with the head at the
crown: t = FS_u p r k_d / (F_tu eta_w), k_d = (a/b)/2, a/b in [1, sqrt(2)] (above sqrt(2) the
equator's hoop stress turns compressive, which a crown check misses: refused); area from the
spheroid formula. Aft domes: p = p_u + rho n g0 H; common dome (LOX side concave, in tension,
assumed): t from FS_u (p_u,LOX + rho_LOX n g0 H_LOX) - p_min,RP1, the relieving side once and
unfactored (SP-10); forward domes: ullage only, unchanged by a push. Transfer tube: hoop at its
bottom from the LOX head over the full LOX column and the tube length minus the RP-1 pressure
outside, diameter assumed.

**Envelope and increment.** The envelope loads and pressures are the pad's, times (1 + margin);
t_env(z) = max over the pad's load cases of t(z, c); t_push(z) = max over the push's load cases
at n_peak. Increment of an element: Delta_m = NOF x sum over stations of 2 pi r rho_w Delta_z
max(0, t_push - t_env) (domes and the tube: area x rho_w x the positive part). NOF by model
fidelity (Wu 2024, coarse models): barrels and skirt central 1.8 (1.54-1.9), domes central 1.9
(1.28-2.76); 1.0 is a unit-cell value that does not apply to this model. Zero inside the
envelope, exactly (a test: the pad's own cases give 0.000 kg). The recorded assisted run's own
flight is checked after the solve against the same envelope; any exceedance is sized and
reported beside dm (not iterated).

**4.2.1 Dynamic load factor.** n_peak = n_0 + DLF (n_q - n_0), F_peak = F_0 + DLF (F_int - F_0);
n_0, F_0 the resting values (g_eff/g0 and m_v g_eff on the carriage; a hold's for a hot start);
n_q the quasi-static plateau. DLF by the case's `dynamic`: `rise_time` min(2, 1 + T/(pi t_r));
`quasi_static` 1; `step` 2. For constant_accel under `rise_time`, n_q is the plateau a drive
ramping over t_r needs to reach the same (L, v_e): a' = [L - sqrt(L^2 - v_e^2 t_r^2 / 12)] /
(t_r^2 / 12) for a linear ramp from rest (SP-8), and the trajectory is not re-flown (stated);
lm_cmd flies the same ramp and checks it. The hydrostatic heads use n_peak. Damping is not
credited. The single-mode factor is applied to every station; it is not conservative at the
upper stations for short rise times (SP-7), and the rows say so.

**4.2.2 Checks reported (flags with numbers, not sized).**
- `structure_release_unload`: for every drive the release is an instant load drop from n_rel
  to n_after. Single-mode swing to n_after - (n_rel - n_after) (and, for a taper, its own ramp
  DLF). Compared with the pad's MECO shutdown under the same treatment (5.195 g0 to 0 on nearly
  empty stage-1 tanks, full stage-2 tanks). Reported per stage-1 tank: the bottom pressure's
  minimum (absolute; negative means the column would separate), the common dome's reverse
  pressure, the upper stack's swing against MECO's.
- `structure_upper_stack_exceeded`: n_peak U above the pad's maximum n U (stage-2 and interstage
  increments are then charged; the flag says so).
- `structure_payload_limit`: n_peak above +6.0 g or the release swing below -2.0 g (Falcon
  User's Guide 2025, Table 5-3, payloads over 1,800 kg; limit loads at the payload interface);
  the payload adapter and the payload are not sized.
- `structure_fixed_point_cap`: the inner iteration hit its cap.

**4.2.3 Load entry (Q5).** Central `aft_ring`: (a) the aft skirt as a stiffened element (the
envelope its hold-down support, central; the flight thrust path as the alternative); (b) a ring
frame at the base carrying the push from N_p equally spaced carriage pads into the skirt: an
interior span of a continuous beam, l = 2 pi r / N_p, under the skirt's uniform reaction q =
F_peak / (2 pi r), M = q l^2 / 12; a rectangular section of aspect h/b; FS_u, F_tu, a fitting
factor; no envelope credit (new hardware; the hold-down hardpoints are not credited, stated);
NOF of the barrels. Row `thrust_structure`: the push enters through the thrust structure;
Delta_m = k_ts T_max max(0, F_peak/T_max - 1), k_ts from the opened thrust-structure relations
(Castellini 2012, 0.20-0.36 kg/kN, central at its midpoint; Akin's 0.255 a quoted cross-check),
T_max = 8,227 kN; the aft skirt and ring are then not loaded by the push. The two rows are
reported with equal prominence.

**4.2.4 Stage 2 and the interstage.** Stage 2's tanks by the same elements and modes against the
pad's full-flight envelope (MECO with full stage-2 tanks, the stage-2 burn); the interstage
(composite) by scaling its mass-estimating-relation mass with the Gerard exponent,
m_is ((N_d,push / N_d,env)^n - 1)+ (an assumption, stated). The stage-2 increment is added to
stage 2's dry mass in the transform (it costs about 24 kg of offload per kg, RQ1/RQ3).

**4.2.5 Plausibility (calibration, not validation).** The model's envelope mass of the sized
stage-1 elements (x NOF) against Heineman's tank relation (+/-30%; Wu 2024), the interstage and
thrust-structure relations and 22.2 t less the engines. Rule (D-SP7-33): if the envelope tank
mass falls below the relation's band, the physics band's adverse end is scaled by the ratio
(relation's lower edge / model); if above, its favourable end by (relation's upper edge /
model). Printed in the Structure subsection and the note.

**4.2.6 The physics band and the axes: the search.** Coefficient groups (counted exactly in S0):
- physics (sourced or physical): E, nu, rho_w, F_tu, eta_w, FS_u, each tank's (p_u, p_min)
  pair (p_min <= p_u), s_dg (0 or 1), k_s, the Gerard row (discrete, with the plate-limited row),
  t_min, L_s, a/b, the densities, the ullage fraction, the skirt envelope path (discrete), the
  ring's h/b and fitting factor, the tube diameter, the stage-2 layout values;
- design assumptions (axes): NOF (barrels, domes), t_r, f, N_p, margin, an envelope cap
  (the pad's stage-1 felt n capped at 4.5 and 4.0 g0 when building the envelope; the trajectory
  stays unthrottled);
- rows, not band: the entry path, the sizing basis, the dynamic mode.

Method (sizing only, at SP1's headline push: constant_accel 3 g0, 100 m, cold, x = 41,262.908 kg,
P_ref, the central design assumptions), frozen in the structure file before any flight:
1. tornado: each physics coefficient at its low and high (each alternative of a discrete one),
   the rest central;
2. a 5-point scan of each continuous physics coefficient: monotone, or not;
3. the monotone ones at their dm-lowering and dm-raising ends; the non-monotone and discrete
   ones enumerated jointly on their scan points (budget <= 2,000 sizings);
4. an extremality check: 500 seeded random interior samples; the largest excess beyond the
   found extremes is recorded;
5. the physics band's low-mass and high-mass sets; the outer envelope the same over both
   groups; the break-even value of each design axis (where the coupled-placement estimate of x*
   falls to 50% and to 0% of 41.263 t).
The search is a pure function in structure.py; a slow test flies the committed pad baseline and
the headline push, re-runs it and asserts the file's sets (S2's gate budgets its wall time:
under 10 min). The file records the parent commit, the test name, the method and the evaluation
count. The label is "the extremes found by the screened search over the stated ranges (method,
evaluations recorded)", never "the bounds". Other pushes (strokes, drives, hot starts) get
central solves; their band ends are sizing-only estimates labelled "the headline's sets".

### 4.4 Coupling, the payload search and the records

**Offload solve.** `OffloadProblem` gains `transform: Callable[[Vehicle, float], Vehicle] |
None = None`, applied in `vehicle_at` after `with_offload`; with None every line of SP1's path
is unchanged. The transform, built in sim.py from the case's start context, the structure
config, the closed-over geometry and the envelope (computed once from the pad baseline's Result;
for an arm, from the pad flown under the same perturbation), maps the vehicle at x and P_ref to
vehicle + dm1 (stage 1) + dm2 (stage 2). constant_accel: the load case is analytic (n constant;
the tank contents at push start; F_int's maximum over the push from the thrust schedule). A
linear-motor push (S4b): flown alone (`ctx.planner(P_ref, final).start(assist, track)`, about
8 ms) with dense output, reduced to the governing samples (maximum n, maximum F_int, maximum head
per tank, the events). Each call starts from dm = 0 and iterates (the push depends on the mass)
until |dm_k+1 - dm_k| < 1e-6 kg, capped at 10 iterations with `structure_fixed_point_cap`
recorded; the inner contraction is measured in S3's and S4b's gates. A test asserts vehicle_at(x)
is bit-identical across call orders and repeats. `sizing: full_load` computes dm once at x = 0.

- Monotonicity: m_res(x, dm(x)) stays decreasing while dm'(x) > -1/E, E = 4.38-4.58 kg of offload
  per kg of dry mass (S03 measured): about -0.22 kg/kg. The probe gives -0.009 to -0.014 kg/kg
  for constant_accel and dm' > 0 under a force-controlled linear motor. A fast test asserts
  dm'(x) > -1/(2 E) and smoothness (differences at 1 kg and 10 g steps agree); the solver's
  `offload_nonmonotone` stays the in-run check; the fixed point (brief 5.5 ii) is the fallback.
- Verification: `offload_solved_run` takes the dm of each stage and a provenance; the vehicle
  dict restates them with `source: "first-order station sizing, configs/structures/<file>,
  set <set>"` and a note (never `assumed`); the verification search and the recorded run fly the
  structure; `xv_dry_mass_delta_kg` then equals dm1 + dm2 (a test, within 1e-6 kg); a penalty
  row's dict stays byte-identical to today's.
- Memo: the structure setting and the envelope's pad trajectory key enter the solve and run memo
  keys (results_io 1174), so a structural case never reuses another solve.

**Payload form** (the variant at its own P* with the structure sized for its full-load push):
dm_0 sized at P_ref, P_1* searched on vehicle + dm_0, dm_1 sized at P_1*, P_2* searched. The
contraction c = |(P_2* - P_1*)/(dm_1 - dm_0)| x |(dm_1 - dm_0)/(P_1* - P_ref)| and the residual
estimate c |P_2* - P_1*| are recorded; a flag when that estimate exceeds 0.5 x
final_payload_xtol_kg or c > 0.1. Recorded as a structure payload record, never under the
offload heading; read beside `_st_full` (both are full-load sizings).

**Records** (metrics.json; every structural run of any kind: solved case, payload case, arm,
sweep point): `structure_charged`, `structure_dm_kg` and `structure_dm_stage2_kg` (at x*),
`structure_dm_x0_kg`, `structure_set`, `structure_sizing`, `structure_dynamic`,

### 4.6 The linear motor (assist/linear_motor.py)

**Config** `LinearMotorConfig` (replaces `PlannedAssistConfig` for that tag; every number strict
and finite): `stroke_m` (> 0), `control` (`force` | `acceleration`), `force_max_kN` (> 0),
`power_max_MW` (> 0), `force_rise_time_s` (>= 0; 0 is a step), `accel_command_g` (> 0, required
for `acceleration`), `stop_taper_m` (>= 0, optional; release at L only), `carriage_mass_t`
(>= 0), `brake_decel_g` (> 0), `drive_efficiency` (0, 1], `exhaust_impingement_fraction`
[0, 1], `shaft` (`vented` | `sealed`, with `shaft_bore_m`, `shaft_gap_m`, `shaft_gamma` only when
sealed, S5), `track`; `release_speed_mps` (optional, > 0, left out of the dump when unset)
arrives in S4a with its event, not before. No assist-family key names. The depth-bound check
(config.py 1433-1438) generalised to any track drive.

**Model** (track equation as today, base.py 11-20: M sddot = F + (1 - f_imp) T - M g_eff sin phi
- F_p(s); F_int = m_v (sddot + g_eff sin phi) - T):
- `force`: F = min(F_lim(t), F_max, P_max / sdot) with F_lim = F_0 + (F_max - F_0) min(1,
  t/t_r) (F_max when t_r = 0; the power term only for sdot > 0); F_0 = max(0, M_0 g_eff sin phi
  - (1 - f_imp) T(0)) fixed at push start through `at_push_start(m_vehicle, thrust)`, which
  returns a bound copy (existing models return themselves).
- `acceleration`: a net acceleration command a(t) = a_cmd min(1, t/t_r) (a jerk-limited profile,
  mass-independent kinematics), F = M (a(t) + g_eff sin phi) + F_p - (1 - f_imp) T, then capped by
  F_max and P_max / sdot (a cap that binds is logged as an event).
- stop taper (release at L): the command or the force limit is scaled by min(1, (L - s) /
  s_taper) toward the holding value over the last s_taper metres.
- P_max is mechanical at the carriage; electricity = drive work / eta and the electrical peak =
  mechanical peak / eta are reported.
- Events: `drive_ramp_end` (the rise's kink time, merged into the sub-phase boundaries; empty for
  existing models, so their call sequence is unchanged); `power_limit` (sdot = P_max/F_max,
  non-terminal, logged); `drive_cap` for the acceleration law.
- Refusals: a stall at push start (F_max + (1 - f_imp) T(0) <= M_0 g_eff sin phi), a failed run
  with its reason; a release-referenced ignition with t_ign_s < 0; ramp starts by depth, speed
  or height (as today for any drive but constant_accel).
- Ignition: `push_time_exact` False, so a release-referenced ignition with t_ign_s >= 0 is
  written as t_release + t_ign_s after the exit, which both planners read unchanged (planar.py
  938, vertical.py 384-388); `flag_ignored_ignition_settings` skips it. `push_time_estimate`
  serves only the step cap.
- Braking functions (D-SP7-12): distance v^2/(2 a_b), time v/a_b, brake force on the carriage
  m_c (a_b - g_eff sin phi), brake energy (1/2) m_c v^2 (1 - g_eff sin phi / a_b), with a_b =
  brake_decel_g g0 the total deceleration; facility = max(L, s_release + d_brake).
- Metrics, conditional on linear_motor (`net_accel_*` stay None as today): `push_force_max_N`,
  `push_power_max_W`, `push_rise_time_s`, `push_corner_speed_mps`, `push_power_limited`,
  `push_accel_mean_mps2` (v_exit / t_push, named a mean), `push_felt_g_plateau`,
  `release_position_m`, `release_trigger`, `electrical_power_peak_W`, `braking_time_s`,
  `brake_force_N`, `brake_energy_J`; a vented-shaft assumption line for linear_motor.
- The 1-D planner takes the drive with no change (S01).

### 4.8 The sealed shaft (S5)

The carriage seals the bore above a gap h_0 of trapped air at the push-start pressure p_0 =
p_atm(z_start): F_p(s) = max(0, p_0 - p_0 (h_0/(h_0 + s))^gamma) A_bore, on the carriage, against
the motion, bounded by p_0 A_bore (1.09 MN at 3.7 m). Its work in a new `PistonWork` record (and
a model extra state `W_piston_J`), outside `AssistEnergyBudget`'s fields, with the energy identity
checked as work_drive + work_thrust = delta_mech + massflow + dissipated + W_piston. Refused: bore
at or below the body diameter. Stated, not modelled: the air pushed out of the mouth above the
vehicle (about 77 kN at exit, 0.4% of F_int; it acts on the vehicle), the gas cooling that makes
the adiabat first order near the end of a small-gap stroke, and that a vented shaft needs vents
of about the bore area (the 3.7 m bore around a 3.66 m body is choked, S07).
