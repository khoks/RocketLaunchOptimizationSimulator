Launch Scene

T+0.00 safter release
Event marks over the time slider.

The launch scene is drawn on a canvas, which this browser does not show. The telemetry and the event list on this page carry the same recorded values as text.

The main view is to one true scale (except the position marker that replaces the vehicle once its body is under the pixel threshold, marked on the view, and the shaft rings, never drawn under 2 px): the site, the vehicle, the shaft, carriage and rails, or the pad mount and clamps, drawn from configs/display and the page's drawing constants (drawn, not computed). The close-up has a scale of its own: the stack shown is drawn at one fixed length, so its scale changes at staging and at the fairing drop; it is not the view's scale. Position, speed, the engines' vacuum thrust, attitude while the model defines one, and propellant are replayed from the recorded time series; nothing is integrated in the browser.

Events

    Each event at the time of its matched row; events marked synthesised are made from the metrics, not logged by the model (the caveats say how).

    Telemetry

    Speed is relative to the Earth. Mass is rebuilt from the two tank series and the stage and fairing flags, as the payload carries them. Engines: the vacuum thrust (thrust_vac_N) over the stage's full vacuum thrust, which sets the plume and the propellant flow; the model's thrust is lower by p_amb A_e near the ground and is 0 N while that exceeds it.

    Read before quoting

      Recorded with this directory's offload block (metrics.json offload.caveats), word for word: written once for the whole block; the items above are built for the runs shown.
