## 1. Context

SP1 answered the headline question on the planar model and built the settings, the offload
solver and an in-memory run path. SP2 makes it something to watch and drive: a local app
(`uv run python -m launchsim app`, 127.0.0.1 only) with a form, a Launch button, job
progress, results with the caveats beside the numbers, a run browser, and an animated 2-D
scene of the pad and the silo launch side by side. SP2 changes no equation, frame, event,
integrator setting, loss accounting, search, guidance or offload solver. Every app run is
exploratory and never a finding.

### 4.4 A2: display geometry and scene payload

- Payload per run (JSON, `allow_nan=False`, ASCII, `<` escaped; token `__SCENE_DATA__`):
  time (6 decimals), altitude, downrange, speed, unwrapped pitch, plume fraction
  (`thrust_vac_N` over the stage's full vacuum thrust of the run's own vehicle block), the
  per-sample step series (stage, phase, fairing on, thrust on), `s_m` and the drive series
  over the push, two tank series (0.1 kg), events with mass before and after each drop,
  assist geometry and push settings (metric first, config fallback for directories from
  before SP1), requested and achieved ramp start, the separated bodies' paths with each
  body's own impact speed and downrange, display geometry, caveats.
- Tank levels: stage-1 rows `prop1 = m - P - d2 - fairing*on - p2 - d1`; stage-2 rows
  `prop2 = m - P - d2 - fairing*on`. P is `payload_kg` of the run's metrics record as
  `run_source` resolves it, else the block's value. Every mass comes from the run's own
  vehicle block. The fill reference is the top-level block for runs, bound runs and offload
  runs, and the entry's own block for cases (a calibration case with its own vehicle would
  otherwise be drawn 3.7% and 13.8% short).
- Load-time checks at every CSV row of every run: the liftoff identity; each tank series
  never rises and has no step above 1 kg at staging or at the fairing time; the stage-2
  tank equals its load before stage-2 ignition and `recorded_m_res_kg` on the last row of a
  flight that reached cutoff or depletion; the tolerances of criterion 5. A run that fails
  still loads and is flagged on the page. A run with an empty time series is left out with
  a one-line reason.
- Events are labelled by name and stage; the pad's liftoff and ramp end are synthesised
  from metrics and marked so; events at one time are stacked.
- Caveats: the scene's display-only list; every item `replay.caveats` generates for the
  selected runs (so the scene's model caveats are a superset of the replay page's, tested);
  the directory's own `offload.caveats`, word for word; the exploratory line.
- Provenance footer: "The vehicle's path, thrust and mass are replayed from the recorded
  time series. The spent stage, fairing halves, carriage after release and unpowered
  attitude are display-only reconstructions and are not model output."
- physics.md display-only section: every formula above, the screen transform
  (x = (R_E + alt) sin(d/R_E), y = (R_E + alt) cos(d/R_E) - R_E; drawn angle = pitch -
  d/R_E, negated on a y-down canvas), the camera law, the held-angle rule.

### 4.5 A3 and A3b: the scene

Scene page contract (fixed before A3 starts):

- Hash parameters, parsed by a pure `parseHash`: `t` (clamped to the data), `theme` (light
  or dark), `runs`, `embed=1`, `autoplay=1`, `qa` (at most 200 times). Nothing from the hash
  is used as HTML, a URL, a selector or a style value.
- `embed=1` keeps the transport, the panels, the telemetry and a one-line caveat strip,
  hides the page's own results table, caveat list and footer, and reports its height to the
  parent.
- Every draw function takes (ctx, box, sceneTime, palette) and reads nothing from the DOM,
  so the same code draws the page, a fixed-size capture canvas and the QA frames. The HUD
  and the caveat footer have canvas versions.
- `window.launchsimScene`: `stateAt(t)` returns per panel the values drawn (altitude,
  downrange, pitch, plume fraction, thrust on, stage, tank fills, rebuilt mass, events
  shown, rate) and what was painted (canvas points of the rocket's base and nose, the
  launch-site ground point, px per metre, close-up length, marker mode); `captureFrame(t)`
  returns a PNG of the composite; `#qa` writes the states as JSON text into one element.

What is drawn, per panel:

- True-scale view: ground, silo cross-section with rings, carriage and brake rails, or the
  pad mount and clamps; the rocket to scale while its body is at least 3 px wide, then a
  marker with a heading tick; the flown path as a trace with event markers; the spent stage
  and fairing halves as hollow markers on dashed paths; a hollow marker for the other
  panel's vehicle; scale bar; Earth's limb when it shows.
- Close-up (fixed scale, the stack about 160 px long, local horizontal level, pitch
  readout): attitude, plume from the plume fraction times thrust on, tank fills, the staging
  gap and the fairing halves opening, each marked "drawn, not computed".
- Tanks: a solid fill with its percentage; the never-loaded band of an offloaded tank is
  hatched for the whole flight and labelled ("S1 89.96% at start, 41.26 t not loaded").
- Camera: a pure function of scene time (H = sqrt(150^2 + (1.25 E)^2) m, E the running
  maximum extent of the shown runs); both panels share scale and pan; a panel whose run has
  ended freezes with a banner ("Ended T+15.7 s: impact").
- Rate: the scene's own law, a pure function of scene time and the shown runs' events: slow
  through the push, the ramp and the kick; a smooth rise to 30x with no step; held near 3x
  around MECO, staging, stage-2 ignition, the fairing drop and cutoff. Options 0.25x to 60x;
  previous-event and next-event buttons and keys; arrow keys; pause when the tab is hidden.
- Tokens: the site's set, light and dark, plus plume, propellant, carriage and steel; every
  shape outlined; a test computes contrast (3:1 shapes, 4.5:1 text) from the template. No
  font links. Fallback text in each canvas. No `innerHTML` from data (a test scans for the
  sinks). An exported page carries a meta Content-Security-Policy and makes no request.
- One-run layout: a single full-width panel. Run selectors, one per panel, grouped by role.
- `launchsim scene <run_dir> [--runs NAME ...] [--out PATH] [--display PATH]`; default
  output `./<experiment>_<timestamp>_scene.html` under the shared output-path rule.

A3 builds the template, the contract, the hook, the page writer, the command, the camera
for one panel, the close-up, and everything from push start to the kick, plus the whole of
silo_failed. A3b builds the marker regime, the trace, MECO, staging, the separated bodies,

### 4.6 A4 and A4b: launch function and server

`run_launch(basis, form, results_root, repo_root, cache, server_start, plots=False)` is a
plain function (A4): `appform` builds the experiment (name `app`, label `exploratory`,
baseline `pad` copied verbatim, at most one variant, the committed energy block);
`resolve_experiment`, `check_result_names`, `check_resolved`; then `git_info`,
`make_run_dir`, the pad (cache or run), the variant, `planar_experiment_result`,
`write_run`; any exception writes FAILED.txt.

Outcomes and what the page shows:

| Outcome | Shown |
|---|---|
| Refused (422) | one line under the field; nothing written |
| Crashed (FAILED.txt) | its last line; no scene |
| Complete, the variant did not fly (failed search or guidance) | "The run did not fly" with the kind; the pad alone |
| Complete, not in orbit (impact, short of orbit) | the scene under a status banner |
| Complete and flagged | the flags above the numbers; a failed verification as "flagged lower bound" |
| Offload found nothing | said so, with the pad control's verdict |

Server (A4b):

- `ThreadingHTTPServer` subclass, IPv4, the constant 127.0.0.1, exclusive binding, handler
  timeout 30 s; only `do_GET` and `do_POST`; silent logging; JSON errors with no traceback;
  `charset=utf-8` on every text response. The cache, the job and the video session belong
  to the server object; the server is a context manager that joins its worker.
- Guard, before any body byte is read: one Host equal to 127.0.0.1:<port> or
  localhost:<port>; Origin absent or the app's own; Sec-Fetch-Site, when present,
  same-origin or none (except on `GET /`); no query string; path split before decoding, each
  segment decoded once.
- Routes: `GET /`; `GET /api/form` (defaults, presets, limits, headline text, boot id);
  `POST /api/launches` (JSON up to 64 KiB; `dry_run: true` returns the derived values or the
  refusal and never starts a job; 202, 409 busy, 422 refused); `GET /api/job`;
  `GET /api/results`; `GET /api/results/<experiment>/<timestamp>`;
  `GET /scene/<experiment>/<timestamp>/<runs>` (1 to 4 distinct runs; one build at a time;
  an LRU of 8 encoded payloads); the video routes of 4.9. Every other path is 404.
- Headers: nosniff, no-store, no-referrer, same-origin resource and opener policy on every
  response; `GET /` is unframeable with a CSP that allows its own script, `connect-src
  'self'` and `frame-src 'self'`; `/scene/...` may be framed by the same origin and can make
  no request; no CORS header anywhere.
- A directory is `<experiment>/<timestamp>`, checked against the results naming rules and
  looked up in the server's own listing; links and oversized files are skipped. Browser
  states: running, complete (playable or not, with the reason), failed, incomplete,
  unreadable. The server never writes into or deletes a directory it did not create.
- Stopping: Ctrl+C and Ctrl+Break are caught; the server closes and writes FAILED.txt for an
  active job.
- Results panel data and the run browser rows are built from the written directory, so a
  recorded directory shows the same panel as a fresh launch.

### 4.7 A5: the app page

- Form: each field has a plain label, its unit, the config key in small type, a default and
  a range. Groups: site (pad only, silo); push (depth; exit speed or net acceleration);
  silo details (carriage mass and exhaust impingement editable; brake 5 g0, drive efficiency
  0.5, vented shaft, vertical track read-only); ramp start (time from release or push start,
  depth, speed, height by event, height by closed form); startup (vehicle default, step,
  ramp, lag); failed ignition; propellant (full load; imposed offload; solve stage 1; under
  Advanced the stage-2 and both forms); stage-2 pre-offload; paired pad; assumed structural
  penalty.
- Disabled states, each with its reason shown: pad only disables the push, the silo details,
  push-relative ramp starts, the offload and the penalty; full load disables the
  pre-offload, the paired pad and the penalty; failed ignition disables the offload; a
  penalty clears the paired pad; a solve locks the pad control on.
- Live derived values from the dry run: exit speed or net acceleration (whichever was not
  typed), push time, felt g, braking distance and facility length, the drag-free apex limit
  for a height, the ramp start as time, depth, speed and height, the run names, the expected
  duration. A refusal marks its field and puts one line under it.
- Presets (the server's copies of the committed fragments; untouched fields are sent as
  stored, so the 200 m exit speed keeps all its digits): pad alone; silo_cold;
  silo_hot_ramp_on_track; silo_cold_200m; silo_cold_s1; fixed 5% and 10%; penalty +2, +4,
  +8.1 t; silo_cold_lag, silo_hot_full, silo_hot_full_impinged, silo_sled_22t, silo_failed,
  silo_instant, pad_instant. First open: silo_cold. The two instant yardsticks are M5 anchor
  names in compare.py; they are offered only if a one-variant launch reproduces the
  screening directory's numbers and flags, otherwise dropped with a logged deviation.
- Launch: the button states the expected duration and "cannot be cancelled"; during a job
  the form, the browser and the current scene stay usable; the progress line shows the
  stage, the elapsed time and the range, and "longer than expected" past it; at completion
  the new scene opens unless the player was touched, else a button offers it.
- Results panel, in order: the tag ("Exploratory app run: not a finding", or the recorded
  directory with its git state); one headline for the launch kind; the comparison line
  ("same configuration and code as SP1's <case>: a reproduction, not new evidence", or what
  differs from SP1's case); the push; the flight, with max-Q "above" or "below the pad's"
  from the run's own metrics; flags and verification; then the caveats for this run. A
  stage-2 or both solve is shown net of its pad control as a property of the vehicle model;
  an imposed stage-2 or both offload says it is not netted.
- Run browser: two groups (app runs, recorded experiments), newest first, each row with a
  one-line description, state, size and git state; a "playable only" filter.
- Keys reach the player from the app page; focus moves into the frame when a scene loads.

### 4.8 A6v: video (first to be deferred if room runs out)

`POST /api/videos` fixes the job (fps from a list, frames = fps x seconds up to 1,800,
width from a list, a 512 MiB budget) and returns an unguessable id;
`POST /api/videos/<id>/frames/<n>` takes `image/png` up to 4 MiB, in order, each checked as
a PNG of exactly the job's size before it is piped; `POST /api/videos/<id>/finish`. ffmpeg
is resolved once at start to an absolute path outside the working directory, the repository
and the results root, run with an argument list and a forced PNG decoder, with timeouts and
clean-up. The file is `<experiment>_<timestamp>_<left>-vs-<right>_scene.mp4` in the working
directory, reserved exclusively, never overwriting; its path is shown on the page. A launch
and a video do not run together. The page shows "frame n of N" and Cancel. Each frame
carries the three lines of `plots.animation_caveats`, the display-only line and, for an app
run, the exploratory line. Capture time and file size are measured before the limits are
fixed.

### 4.9 What the scene shows that the model does not support

Named on the page, in the manual and in the gallery entry:

- Every shape and length. The body attitude (the model has a thrust direction; unpowered
  attitude is held). `alt_m` is drawn at the rocket's base.
- The spent stage and the fairing halves: a drag-free coast shown with each run's own
  computed impact speed and downrange. The coast stays within 0.2 m of the vehicle's
  recorded path during the 11 s staging coast, so the gap drawn then is a drawing choice;
  both fairing halves follow one path; a body still in flight when the run ends is drawn
  stopped there.
- The plume inside the shaft and the carriage beneath lit engines: the model has a vented
  shaft and one impingement fraction that moves only track forces and drive energy.
- The carriage after release and where it brakes. A failed ignition falls back along an
  obstacle-free path: the model has no contact with the carriage, the mouth or the shaft.
- One propellant level per stage; tanks read empty at depletion (no residual or reserve).
- The pad's liftoff marker (the model logs release); the marker that replaces the rocket.

## 6. Exit criteria (tolerances fixed)

1. The app starts: 127.0.0.1 only, URL printed, a second server on the same port fails.
2. A launch works for: exit speed; net acceleration; each of the five ramp-start ways; a
   solved stage-1 offload; an imposed offload; a stage-2 offload with its pad control; a
   structural penalty; the screening presets. Each writes a normal results directory whose
   resolved config holds the form's values, and its scene plays, or the page says why there
   is none.
3. Same numbers as the CLI: the silo_cold_s1 preset launched from the app gives the payload
   capacity and offload of results/silo_offload_2d/20261003T112934Z within 0.002 kg.
4. Refusals: each bad input of section 5.7 gives a one-line message and writes nothing;
   1e400, a 400-digit integer, `true` and "3" in a number field are refused; a second
